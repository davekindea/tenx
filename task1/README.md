# 🌞 Solar Farm Data Analysis Challenge 🚀  

## 📖 **Project Overview**  
This project focuses on **analyzing and understanding solar farm data** provided by **MoonLight Energy Solutions**.  
The goal is to perform a quick analysis of solar radiation measurement data and generate insights to support **strategic decisions for solar investments**.  
The analysis will identify **high-potential regions** for solar installation aligned with the company’s **long-term sustainability goals**.  

---

## 🎯 **Business Objective**  
MoonLight Energy Solutions aims to enhance **operational efficiency** and **sustainability** through **targeted solar investments**.  
As an **Analytics Engineer**, your task is to:  
- Analyze environmental measurements  
- Identify key trends  
- Develop a **strategy report** to help make **data-driven decisions**  

The analysis will consider factors such as:  
- Solar radiation  
- Temperature  
- Humidity  
- Wind speed  
- Precipitation  

---

## 📊 **Dataset Overview**  
The dataset contains **solar radiation measurement data** along with various **environmental parameters**.  
Below are the key attributes:  

- **Timestamp (yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm)**: Date and time of observation  
- **GHI (W/m²)**: Global Horizontal Irradiance  
- **DNI (W/m²)**: Direct Normal Irradiance  
- **DHI (W/m²)**: Diffuse Horizontal Irradiance  
- **ModA (W/m²)**: Module A measurements  
- **ModB (W/m²)**: Module B measurements  
- **Tamb (°C)**: Ambient Temperature  
- **RH (%)**: Relative Humidity  
- **WS (m/s)**: Wind Speed  
- **WSgust (m/s)**: Maximum Wind Gust Speed  
- **WSstdev (m/s)**: Wind Speed Standard Deviation  
- **WD (°N)**: Wind Direction  
- **WDstdev**: Wind Direction Standard Deviation  
- **BP (hPa)**: Barometric Pressure  
- **Cleaning (1 or 0)**: Indicates sensor/module cleaning status  
- **Precipitation (mm/min)**: Precipitation rate  
- **TModA (°C)**: Module A Temperature  
- **TModB (°C)**: Module B Temperature

## ⚙️ **Methodology**  

### 1️⃣ **Data Cleaning and Preprocessing**  
- Handle **missing or erroneous values**  
- Convert **timestamps** into usable formats  
- Normalize or scale relevant features  

### 2️⃣ **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**  
- Analyze **distributions of key variables**  
- Perform **correlation analysis**  
- Create **visualizations** (heatmaps, scatter plots, time series analysis)  

### 3️⃣ **Statistical Analysis**  
- Calculate **mean, median, and variance**  
- Analyze trends in **solar radiation, temperature, wind speed, and humidity**  
- Use **hypothesis testing** to validate assumptions  

### 4️⃣ **Identifying High-Potential Regions**  
- Combine environmental data to identify the **best regions for solar installations**  
- Account for **seasonality and trends**  

### 5️⃣ **Strategy Report**  
- Deliver a comprehensive report with **actionable insights**  
- Highlight **key regions for solar installations**  
- Provide suggestions for further analysis
